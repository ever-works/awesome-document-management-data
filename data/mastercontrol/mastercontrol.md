## Overview

MasterControl is a document management system designed for regulated industries. It helps organizations stay compliant with federal regulations including 21 CFR Part 11 requirements for medical, pharmaceutical, and food and beverage sectors.

## Features

- 21 CFR Part 11 compliance
- Time-stamp audit trail
- Reporting capabilities
- eSignature functionality
- Access control
- Versioning
- Training modules for compliance
- Security features for protecting sensitive information
