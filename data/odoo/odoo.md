## Overview

Odoo is an open-source suite of business applications (ERP, CRM, e-commerce, and more) that includes document management capabilities integrated with business records and processes.

## Features

- Document storage and attachment to business records (invoices, projects, contacts, etc.)
- Version control and access permissions
- Folder structure and tagging
- Integration with Odoo CRM, ERP, invoicing, project, and website modules
- Web-based interface
- Multi-company support
- Mobile access

## Deployment

Available as self-hosted (Community Edition, GNU LGPL) and cloud-hosted (Odoo Online, SaaS).

## Pricing

Community Edition is free and open source (GNU LGPL); Enterprise Edition and SaaS hosting are paid.
