## Overview

OpenDocMan is a free, open-source document management system (DMS) written in PHP, designed for small to medium businesses.

## Features

- Document tracking and management
- Version control
- Role-based access control and permissions
- Full-text search
- Web-based installation and administration
- Multi-language support
- Check-in/check-out document locking

## Deployment

Self-hosted; requires a LAMP stack (Linux, Apache, MySQL, PHP).

## Pricing

Free and open source under the GNU General Public License (GPL).
