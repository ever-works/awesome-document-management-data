## Overview

Mygate serves as a secure digital repository for society documents, including invoices, audit reports, NOCs, circulars, and agreements. Files are tagged, version-controlled, and accessible based on admin permissions, making audits and handovers effortless.

## Key Features

- Document repository with tagging and version control
- Admin permission-based access control
- Parking management
- Payment gateways and online payments
- Accounting management
- Invoices
- Help desk
- Child security
- SMS notifications
- Visitor management
- Maintenance communication
- Resident request tracking

## Deployment

Suitable for StartUps, SMBs, Mid-Market, and Enterprises.
