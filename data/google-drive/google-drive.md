## Overview

Google Drive is a cloud storage platform that offers document management features including real-time collaboration, version tracking, and commenting. It integrates with Google Workspace and thousands of third-party apps.

## Features

- Intuitive and easy to use
- Real-time collaboration with multiple team members
- Versioning and change tracking
- Commenting on documents
- Integration with Google Workspace
- Third-party app integrations
- Gemini AI for AI search and AI-powered security
- Document summarization
- eSign option
- Zapier integration for workflow automation

## Pricing

- Free for personal use
- Paid Google Workspace plans start at $7/user/month (annual billing)

## Deployment

Cloud-based platform.
