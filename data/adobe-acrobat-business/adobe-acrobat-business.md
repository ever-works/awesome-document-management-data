## Overview

Adobe Acrobat for Business is a document management solution for businesses that rely heavily on forms and PDF documents. It combines Adobe Acrobat and Acrobat Sign for creating, editing, sharing, signing, and collaborating on PDF files.

## Features

- Strong PDF management capabilities
- User-friendly interface
- Solid mobile app
- Cross-device compatibility
- Create, edit, share, sign, and collaborate on PDF files
- Adobe Acrobat Sign integration
- Multiple file type storage (converted to PDF for editing)

## Pricing

From $14.99/month/license (annual billing, paid monthly)

## Deployment

Cloud-based platform with desktop and mobile applications.
