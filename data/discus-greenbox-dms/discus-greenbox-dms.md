## Overview

Discus Greenbox is a Document Management System (DMS) designed to streamline document processes with advanced integrated Business Process Management (BPM). It focuses on transforming how businesses manage documents rather than just storing them.

## Key Features

- Notifications And Alerts
- Workflow Automation
- Version Control
- Customizable Templates
- Cloud Storage
- Integration Capabilities
- Compliance Management
- Document Capture
