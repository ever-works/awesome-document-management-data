## Overview

Zoho WorkDrive is a cloud-based document management and file collaboration platform designed for teams already using Zoho applications. It enables teams to store and manage files in a centralized repository with granular access controls to ensure sensitive information is handled by authorized users.

## Key Features

- Centralized file storage and team folders
- Granular access controls and permissions
- Version control to prevent accidental overwrites
- Built-in eSigning capabilities
- Integration with popular office suites and Zoho ecosystem apps
- Zapier integration for connecting with thousands of other business apps
- Intuitive search functionality for finding documents

## Pricing

- Free plan available
- Paid plans from $2.50/user/month (billed annually), minimum of 3 users

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS)
