## Overview

Hyland OnBase is an enterprise content management (ECM) platform for managing documents, records, and business processes at scale.

## Features

- Document imaging and capture
- Records management and retention
- Workflow and business process automation
- Case management
- Full-text search and retrieval
- Role-based access control
- Integrations with ERP, CRM, and line-of-business systems

## Deployment

Available as on-premise and cloud-hosted deployment options.

## Pricing

Commercial, enterprise-grade software with pricing based on deployment scope and user count.
