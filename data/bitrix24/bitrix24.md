## Overview

Bitrix24 is an all-in-one platform that includes document management software alongside CRM and collaboration tools.

## Features

- Email Marketing
- Mobile CRM
- Document Management
- Workflow Automation
- Sales Automation
- Telephony Integration
- Task Management
- Reporting And Analytics
- File sharing
- Version tracking

## Pricing

- **Free**: $0 per user/month
- **Plus**: $39 per month for unlimited users
- **Standard**: $99 per month for unlimited users
- **Professional**: $199 per month for unlimited users
