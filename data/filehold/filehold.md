## Overview

FileHold is a commercial document management system (DMS) for small to mid-sized businesses and organizations.

## Features

- Document capture and scanning
- Version control and check-in/check-out
- Full-text search
- Workflow automation
- Records management and retention schedules
- Role-based security and permissions
- Microsoft Office integration
- Email management (Outlook integration)

## Deployment

Available as on-premise (self-hosted) and cloud-hosted editions.

## Pricing

Commercial, paid software licensed per user or per server.
