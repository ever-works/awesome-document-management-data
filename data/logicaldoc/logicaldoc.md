## Overview

LogicalDOC is a document management system (DMS) for managing, indexing, and sharing business documents. It is offered in both a free community edition and commercial editions.

## Features

- Full-text search across documents
- Version control and document history
- Workflow automation
- Role-based access control and permissions
- Document indexing and metadata management
- Multi-language support
- Web-based user interface

## Deployment

Self-hosted; available as a community edition and commercial editions.

## Pricing

Community edition is open source (GNU LGPL); commercial editions are paid with licensing based on deployment scale.
