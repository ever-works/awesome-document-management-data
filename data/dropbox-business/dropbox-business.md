## Overview

Dropbox Business is a cloud storage service that also serves as a lightweight document management solution. It offers versioning for collaboration, eSignature, and audit logs as document management features.

## Features

- User-friendly interface
- Seamless integration with popular office suites
- Secure file storage and sharing
- Link expiration
- Versioning for collaboration
- eSignature
- Audit logs
- Zapier integration for workflow automation

## Pricing

- Free plan available for personal use
- Business plans start at $16.58/month (annual billing) for a single user
- $15/user/mo for 3+ users

## Deployment

Cloud-based platform.
