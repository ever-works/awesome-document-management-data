## Overview

eFileCabinet is a document management system (DMS) designed for small and medium-sized businesses to capture, store, and manage digital documents.

## Features

- Document capture and scanning
- Secure document storage and retrieval
- Version control
- Workflow automation
- Electronic signatures (RightSignature)
- Role-based access control
- Full-text search with OCR
- Integrations with Microsoft Office and Outlook

## Deployment

Available as cloud-hosted and on-premise (self-hosted) editions.

## Pricing

Commercial, paid software with subscription pricing; pricing varies by deployment model and user count.
