## Overview

ONLYOFFICE is an open-source office suite and document collaboration platform offering online document editors, version control, sharing, and access management.

## Features

- Document, spreadsheet, and presentation editors
- Real-time co-authoring and collaboration
- Version control and document history
- Access control and sharing permissions
- Comments, chat, and review modes
- Form creation and filling
- Integration with Nextcloud, ownCloud, SharePoint, and other DMS/ECM platforms

## Deployment

Available as self-hosted (Community Edition, GNU AGPL) and cloud (SaaS) editions.

## Pricing

Community Edition is free and open source (GNU AGPL); commercial editions and SaaS subscriptions are paid.
