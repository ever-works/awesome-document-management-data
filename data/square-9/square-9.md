## Overview

Square 9 provides a range of document management solutions for different business needs. Its GlobalAction workflow automation lets you map document activities from data capture to archiving, with workflows for extracting information from external and internal documents.

## Features

- GlobalAction workflow automation
- Sharp OCR for scanning large volumes of documents
- Intuitive and fast search functionality
- Workflow creation for data extraction from internal and external documents
- Document routing throughout the organization
- Disposal and retention management for confidential information
- Document capture tool for high-volume documents including emails and attachments

## Pricing

From $50/user/month (minimum 5 users)

## Deployment

Cloud-based platform.
